EVESup: live previews and client switching for EVE Online on Mac
======================================================================

INSTALL

1. Drag EVESup into the Applications folder.

2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software,
   because it isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer.
   Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to the EVESup message.
   (Or run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/EVESup.app)

3. EVESup lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon). On first launch it opens its
   settings on the General page. Allow Screen Recording there: it's needed to
   read character names and show previews. Then click "Relaunch EVESup".


USE

- Cycle Groups: click +, name the group, click "Next client" / "Previous client"
  and press a key, then add characters in the order you want to cycle.
- Character Keys: one key that jumps straight to a character.
- Previews: click a preview to switch to it, drag to move, drag the bottom-right
  corner to resize, shift-click to skip that client while cycling.
- Settings Sync: copy one character's or account's EVE settings over the others.
  A backup is made first, and "Restore Backup" puts it back. EVE must be closed.

Tip: set EVE's Window Mode to "Fixed Window" for game windows without a title bar.


NOTES

- Requires macOS 14 or later.
- EVESup only shows previews and brings EVE windows to the front. It never
  sends keystrokes or mouse clicks to EVE and doesn't modify the game.
